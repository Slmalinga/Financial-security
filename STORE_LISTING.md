## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Take control of your money with FinSight — a clean, private budgeting app built around the proven 50/30/20 rule. Track your spending, plan every dollar, and get AI-powered insights, all from a beautifully simple dashboard.

FinSight is **local-first**: your financial data is stored on your own device, not on our servers. There are no accounts to create and no sign-up required — just open the app and start.

**📊 See your money clearly**
A live dashboard shows your safe-to-spend balance, total outflow, and income target at a glance, with visual charts that break spending down by category.

**🎯 Budget with the 50/30/20 rule**
Split your income across Needs, Wants, and Savings, set your own percentages, and instantly see whether your plan is balanced. Map planned items to each bucket and know the moment a category goes over its limit.

**✨ AI-powered insights**
Get concise, actionable financial guidance based on your spending. FinSight analyzes your budget against the 50/30/20 framework and suggests where to adjust. (This optional feature sends a summary of your spending to Google's Gemini AI for processing — see our privacy policy.)

**➕ Fast, flexible tracking**
Log transactions in seconds, organize them with fully customizable categories, and review your recent activity in a clean history feed.

**🔄 You own your data**
Export a full backup of your data anytime, migrate it between devices, or permanently wipe everything from your device with one tap. No lock-in, no hidden cloud.

**Why FinSight?**
• No account, no login, no ads
• No selling of your personal data
• Works on any modern device
• Simple enough for daily use, powerful enough to keep you on track

Please note: FinSight is a personal budgeting and money-management tool. It does not provide banking, lending, payments, or investment services.

Start building better money habits today with FinSight.

## Listing notes

- The Gemini AI data processing is disclosed in the description on purpose —
  Google checks for consistency between the listing, the privacy policy
  (`public/privacy.html`), and the Data safety form.
- The "not banking/lending/investment" line aligns with the financial-features
  declaration in the Play Console.
- Avoided "encrypted", "secure vault", "best", and testimonial/guarantee
  language, which are common rejection triggers.
