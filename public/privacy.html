Privacy Policy for FinSight

Last updated: 5 September 2026

FinSight ("the app", "we", "us") is a personal finance and budgeting tool that follows a local-first design. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. By using FinSight you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information you enter

FinSight lets you record financial information such as transactions, amounts, descriptions, categories, monthly income, and budget allocations.

  • This data is stored only on your own device, in your browser's local storage. We do not operate accounts, and we do not upload, sync, or back up your financial records to any server we control.
  • You can export your data (as a plain-text JSON file) or permanently erase all of it at any time using the in-app Purge feature.

2. AI insights and third-party processing

When you use the optional AI insights feature, FinSight sends a summary of your spending — including recent transaction descriptions and amounts, your income figure, and category totals — to Google's Gemini API in order to generate budgeting suggestions.

  • This request is made through our own secure endpoint (/api/advice); your Google/Gemini API key is never exposed to other users.
  • The data is processed by Google under their own terms. See Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy and the Gemini API terms at https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms.
  • We do not store the content of these AI requests on our servers.
  • If you do not use the AI insights feature, none of your financial data leaves your device.

3. Information we do NOT collect

  • No user accounts, names, email addresses, or passwords.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • No location data.
  • No device contacts, photos, or files.

4. Data security

Because data is stored locally in your browser, its security depends on the security of your device. Note that local data and exported backup files are not encrypted. Keep any exported backup files somewhere safe, and use your device's own lock screen and security features.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it. You can remove all data instantly with the in-app Purge feature, or by clearing the app's storage / uninstalling the app. Since we do not hold your data on a server, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on your behalf.

6. Children's privacy

FinSight is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
