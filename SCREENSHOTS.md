# Play Store Screenshot Captions — FinSight

Play has no separate caption field — these are the short **text overlays** you
place on top of each screenshot image. Ordered to tell a story; Google shows
the first few most prominently.

Requirements: at least 2 phone screenshots (5–8 ideal), each 320–3840 px per
side, 16:9 or 9:16.

---

## 1. Dashboard
- **Headline:** See your money at a glance
- **Subtext:** Balance, spending, and income in one clean view

## 2. Allocation chart (Dashboard)
- **Headline:** Know where every dollar goes
- **Subtext:** Visual breakdowns by category, updated live

## 3. Budget Planner (50/30/20)
- **Headline:** Budget with the 50/30/20 rule
- **Subtext:** Split income across Needs, Wants & Savings

## 4. Category limits / planned items
- **Headline:** Stay on track, automatically
- **Subtext:** Instant alerts when a category goes over

## 5. AI insight card
- **Headline:** AI-powered money insights
- **Subtext:** Smart, actionable tips based on your spending

## 6. Add Transaction
- **Headline:** Log spending in seconds
- **Subtext:** Fast entry with fully custom categories

## 7. Sync / Backup
- **Headline:** Your data, your device
- **Subtext:** Export a backup anytime — no account needed

---

## Design tips
- Keep overlay text in the **top third** so it clears the phone UI in the frame.
- Use brand colors: navy `#1E2B58`, teal `#4AA3A3`, gold `#C59D5F`.
- Lead with screenshots **1, 3, and 5** — the strongest hooks, and the ones
  Google surfaces first.
- 5–8 screenshots is ideal; the minimum is 2.
